Prayer Sealed Launching December 2026

Because Prayer Can’t Wait™

Write a prayer, keep a request, record an answer. Everything is encrypted on your phone before it goes anywhere, so only you and the people you choose to share with can read it. We can’t.

Read more about Prayer SealedShow less

Prayer Sealed is being built to remain free to use, without advertising and without selling users’ personal information. It is designed to help believers pray for others, remember answered prayer, reflect on God’s faithfulness, and participate in prayer with Christian community.

  • End-to-end encryption, built on post-quantum cryptography
  • Share a request with a prayer partner or a group
  • Keep a record of prayers answered

Prayer Sealed is already in private beta. iPhone testing is available through TestFlight by invitation. If you would like to try the app and help us prepare for the planned December 2026 public launch, email us to request a beta invitation.

My Life with Christ In development

A Scripture journal that never leaves your phone.

Read through the Bible by book, by outline, or by topic, and attach your own notes to any of it: sermons you heard, sermons you found, reflections from your own reading, prayers. No account. No server. No network calls at all.

  • Browse Scripture three ways
  • Attach sermon notes, reflections, and prayers to any passage
  • Your journal stays on your device

The iPhone and Android apps are still in development. You can start journaling today with the web companion, which keeps everything in your browser: journal.prayersealed.org.
