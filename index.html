Because Prayer Can’t Wait™

About the ministry

Prayer Sealed Ministries, Inc. is a Florida not-for-profit corporation, incorporated September 25, 2026. It exists to advance Christian prayer and the study of Scripture by building and maintaining devotional software, and by making that software broadly available.

Prayer Sealed Ministries seeks to expand access to the Christian practice of prayer by providing believers with private, freely available digital tools for prayer, reflection, remembrance of answered prayer, and Christian community.

Digital technology increasingly mediates daily life, yet many platforms are built around advertising, engagement metrics, public sharing, and collection of personal information. Prayer Sealed is designed around a different premise: technology should support prayer without distracting from it, commercializing it, or requiring Christians to turn private spiritual life into social-media content.

The technology is the means. Prayer, Christian fellowship, and remembering God’s faithfulness are the ministry.
