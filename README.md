# prayersealed.org

Website for Prayer Sealed Ministries, Inc., a Florida not-for-profit
corporation (document no. N26000013210, incorporated September 25, 2026).

## About the ministry

Prayer Sealed Ministries advances Christian prayer and the study of
Scripture by building devotional software that keeps a person's spiritual
life private.

- **Prayer Sealed** — an encrypted prayer app. What you write stays
  between you and God.
- **My Life with Christ** — a Scripture journal that lives entirely on
  your own device. No account, no server, nothing sent anywhere.

Most devotional software works the other way around.
